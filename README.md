# What's Wrong With My Career?

*by Career Agility International*

A quick, guided career diagnostic you run in conversation with Claude. CoachCAROL, Career Agility International's coaching persona, asks you two to four multiple-choice questions about what's going on in your career. It then gives you one of 43 result cards explaining what's most likely going on, why it matters, three practical things you can do, and one strategic question to reflect on. It takes about two minutes.

It covers seven starting points:

1. I don't know what's next for me
2. I think my career is stalled
3. I'm worried about job security
4. I feel burnt out or overloaded
5. I'm having trouble with someone at work
6. I'm worried my skills aren't good enough
7. I'm having trouble landing a job (applications, interviews, offers, career switches)

The advice draws on the frameworks in *Your 7 Career Currencies* by Adrian Choo and Sze-Yen Chee: the 7 Career Currencies, the Career Crossover Quadrants and the six Career Moves.

## How to start

Install the plugin, then say something like:

- "What's wrong with my career?"
- "Run the career diagnostic."
- "Why am I not landing a job?"
- "I keep getting interviews but never the offer."

After your result, you can talk through any of the actions or run the diagnostic again for a second issue.

## What it runs, sends and stores

- **Runs:** instructions and reference text only. There are no scripts, programs or hooks.
- **Sends:** nothing. It makes no network calls and connects to no outside services.
- **Stores:** nothing. It doesn't save your answers, ask for your name or contact details, or keep any record outside your normal Claude conversation.
- **Contact:** each result ends with one line saying you can email info@careeragility.org for more advice. Emailing is entirely up to you; the plugin never sends anything on your behalf.
